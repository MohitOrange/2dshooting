Neon Brawl – Privacy Policy

Developer: MohitGames · App: Neon Brawl (Android, package com.mohitgames.neonbrawl) · Effective date: 2 November 2026

This policy explains what information the game Neon Brawl ("the game", "we") uses, why, and the choices you have. We keep data use to what the game needs to run, to let you play online with friends, and to show ads that keep the game free.

1. Summary

  • We do not ask for your real name, email, phone number or contacts.
  • Your progress (coins, gems, unlocks, settings) is stored on your device.
  • Online play uses an anonymous player ID from Unity Gaming Services.
  • Voice chat is off by default. When you turn the mic on, your voice is streamed live to players in your room and is not recorded by us.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob (with Unity Ads through AdMob mediation). Personalized ads are optional.
  • Purchases go through Google Play. We never see your card details.

3. Ads and your choices

Neon Brawl is free and shows ads: optional reward videos you choose to watch (for example double coins), an occasional full-screen ad between offline matches, and a small banner on the results screen. Buying Remove Ads turns off the full-screen and banner ads.

  • European Economic Area, UK and Switzerland: on first launch Google's consent form asks whether ads may be personalized. You can change your answer any time in Settings → Privacy → Ad choices.
  • Everywhere: turn off Settings → Privacy → Personal ads to ask our ad partners for non-personalized ads (you will still see ads).
  • You can also reset or delete your Android advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Our ad partners' policies: How Google uses information from apps that use its services, Unity privacy policy.

4. Service providers

  • Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform (ads and consent) – Google Privacy Policy
  • Unity Ads (through AdMob mediation), Unity Gaming Services (Authentication, Lobby, Relay, Multiplayer, Friends) and Vivox (voice chat) – Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy
  • Google Play (download, purchases, crash reports) – Google Privacy Policy

We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared with these providers only so the game can work as described above.

5. Children

Neon Brawl is a cartoon action game made for teens and adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are requested as not child-directed and limited to a Teen content rating. If you believe a child has used online features and want data removed, contact us (section 8).

6. Keeping and deleting data

  • Data saved on your device is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its app data.
  • Your anonymous Unity player ID is kept by Unity while it is in use. To ask for its deletion, contact us with the player name you used; we will forward the request to Unity.
  • We do not keep chat logs or voice recordings.

7. Security

Online traffic to Unity and Google services uses encrypted connections (TLS). No method of transfer or storage is 100% secure, but we use the security provided by these established platforms and collect as little as we can.

8. Contact

Questions, data requests or reports: open an issue at github.com/MohitOrange/2dshooting/issues, or email the developer address shown on the game's Google Play page.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Important changes will also be mentioned in the game's update notes.
